How much TB can disruption and migration generate?

Two mechanisms that require no immune dysfunction: disrupted latent-TB preventive treatment, and growth in the size and source-country composition of the arriving population. Pick the excess you want to explain from the manuscript's six counterfactual specifications, set every parameter yourself, and read off how many cases each mechanism produces. Every constant is a slider; nothing is hidden in the code.

The excess to be explained
Arm 1 · LTBI treatment disruption
Excess arises because people who would have completed preventive therapy did not. Raising coverage, completion or efficacy raises the excess, because more protection was withdrawn.
Arm 2 · Migration volume and composition
Only arrivals above the pre-pandemic baseline can generate excess, because the population offset and the catchment's already-immigrant-heavy baseline rate absorb the rest.
